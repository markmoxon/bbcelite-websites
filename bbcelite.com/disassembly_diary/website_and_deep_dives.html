31 January 2026
================

Between 31 January and 4 April 2026, I wrote 51 deep dives into The Sentinel. That's a lot of content, so I kept track of progress in a text file.

Each article went through a few stages: I wrote the initial draft, then added images and ran an initial spellcheck. I then added deep dive headers into the source code, proofread the article once again, ran a second spellcheck and published the results on the project website.

Here's the completed progress sheet. I filled in an "x" for each stage, working through each article in order. The asterisks show article complexity, while the plus signs indicate articles with more challenging graphical needs. I released the articles in batches, and the dates and article counts on the right show the release dates for each batch.
