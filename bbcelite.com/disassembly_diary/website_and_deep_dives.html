Retrospective diary entry:

Between 30 January and 14 April 2026, I wrote 51 deep dives into The Sentinel.
That's a lot of content, so I kept track of progress in a text file.

Each article went through a few stages: I wrote the initial draft, then added
images and ran an initial spellcheck. I then added deep dive headers into the
source code, proofread the article once again, ran a second spellcheck and
published the results on the project website.
