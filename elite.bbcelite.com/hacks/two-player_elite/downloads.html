How to download and play two-player Elite

You can play two-player Elite by choosing one of the following:

  • Play two-player Elite in your browser.
  • Download two-player Elite as a disc image that will run in an emulator, or on a real BBC Micro or BBC Master 128 using a device like a Gotek.

To load two-player Elite on a BBC, simply insert the disc image and press SHIFT-D-BREAK (for DFS), SHIFT-M-BREAK (for MMFS), SHIFT-S-BREAK (for Micro SPI) or use the *DBOOT command (for all SD card solutions); emulators tend to map BREAK to F12, so press SHIFT-D-F12. The disc will automatically load if you're playing it in a browser.

The controls for player 1 in two-player Elite are identical to standard BBC Micro Elite. For details, you can read the original game manual on Ian Bell's website. For information on player 2's controls and all the various joystick options, check out the instructions.

Hardware requirements
---------------------

You will need a BBC Micro, a BBC Micro B+ or a BBC Master, fitted with a 6502 Second Processor.

For one human player, this is all you'll need, though you can add a joystick or Delta 14B if you prefer.

For two human players, you'll need at least one joystick, but twin joysticks are even better. One or two Delta 14Bs are also supported.

Ultra-fast co-processors, such as those available with the PiTubeDirect, will work fine. The game is frame-limited to be playable on all CPU speeds, but faster co-processors will reduce slow-down when things are busy.
