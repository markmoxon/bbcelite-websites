Bringing all the best Elite hacks together in five feature-packed discs

The Elite Compendium is a collection of the very best of 6502 Elite, updated with as many hacks and enhancements as I've been able to fit in. If you're looking to play 8-bit Elite, then this is a pretty good place to start.

Main enhancements in the Compendium versions of Elite
-----------------------------------------------------

All the extra features and enhancements in the Compendium versions of Elite have been backported from other 6502 versions of the game, so everything is part of the official Elite canon. These are the most important extras that you get in the Compendium:

FeatureDetailsTaken from
Flicker-free graphicsI've added flicker-free ships and planets using the improved ship-drawing algorithm from the Apple II, so the game looks much smoother and more beautiful (read more about flicker-free Elite)Apple II
MusicI've added the music from the Commodore 64 version, with two different tunes and options to swap tunes, disable music and change the volume (read more about musical Elite)Commodore 64
Volume controlI've added volume control for all sound effects (pause the game and use "<" and ">" to change the volume of music and sound effects)BBC Master
Trumbles missionFor the Acorn versions of Elite I've backported the non-sprite version of the Trumbles mission from the NES version (read more about the Trumbles mission)NES
Docking computer improvements

I've backported a couple of docking computer improvements from the NES version, to make auto-docking a bit more enjoyable:

  • The station no longer spawns traders, like Transporters or Shuttles, while the docking computer is activated
  • Once you're inside the station's safe zone and you've activated your docking computer, you can press "J" to dock instantly
NES
Moving fuel scoopsFuel scoops will only work when you are moving, which makes a lot more senseNES
Joystick fixesWhen joysticks are enabled, the keyboard is no longer disabled, so you can have one hand on the stick and one on the keyboardBBC Master

All of the Compendium versions of Elite contain all of the above enhancements, with the following exceptions:

  • The Compendium version of BBC Micro cassette Elite doesn't include the Trumbles mission, as this version doesn't include any missions at all.
  • The Compendium version of Commodore 64 Elite doesn't support the Delta 14B joystick as it's only available for Acorn machines.
  • The Compendium version of Acorn Electron Elite doesn't include volume control as the hardware can't support it, and an E00 filing system is required to support music and the Delta 14B joystick.

These are the main features of the Compendium, but there's more, so let's look at exactly what you get in each Compendium version.

The Compendium versions of Firebird Elite work on the unexpanded Commodore 64 and Commodore 128.

For Acornsoft Elite, extra memory is required by all of these Elite hacks, so the Compendium versions need either a BBC Master 128, a BBC Micro with 16K of sideways RAM, or an Acorn Electron with 16K of sideways RAM. You will also need a 6502 co-processor if you want to play the 6502 Second Processor version of Elite.

Apart from the Elite Universe Editor, the use of teletext in Teletext Elite, and Delta 14B support, all of the hacks included in the Compendium are 100% canon - they are all taken from official versions of 6502 Elite, as noted above (and Delta 14B support is from Elite-A, which is as close to canon as it's possible to get). This means they were all written by Elite's authors, Ian Bell and David Braben (with Aidan Bell and Julie Dunn composing the music). So the flicker-free drawing algorithm is from Apple II Elite, the music comes from the Commodore 64 version, the docking computer enhancements are from the NES version, red enemy lasers are from the 6502 Second Processor version, and so on.

So the Compendium is effectively a Greatest Hits of the original Elite, and in terms of gameplay, nothing new has been added; it's all original Bell and Braben brilliance, just backported to work on the BBC Master and BBC Micro.

What else is on the disc?
-------------------------

The Compendium also contains three commander files on drive 0, which you can load into any version of Elite via the save and load menu (press "Y" on the title screen or press "@" when docked):

  • MISS1 is for those who want to play the Constrictor mission; load it, launch from the station, dock once again (you have a docking computer) and the mission will start.
  • MISS2 is for those who want to play the Thargoid Plans mission, and you can trigger it in the same way.
  • MAX is a maxed-out commander to let you hit the ground running.

All of these commanders will also trigger the Trumbles mission when you dock:

If you load MISS1 or MISS2, then the Trumble mission will be offered on your second docking, as the first docking will trigger the missions shown above; if you load MAX, then the Trumbles will be offered on your first docking. Remember that the Compendium implements instant docking with the docking computer, so the quickest way to trigger a mission is to load the relevant commander file, then press f0 to launch, "C" to activate the docking computer, "J" to insta-dock, and then you will be offered the mission.
